Privacy Policy

Nothing to Lose · Last updated 29 September 2026

The short version

Nothing to Lose does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, the names you choose for your character and partner, and your settings are saved only on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personal data of any kind.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not include third-party services that collect data.
  • The app does not need an internet connection and does not send anything over the network.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

Children

The game contains mature themes (crime, violence, gambling, drug references and strong language) and is rated for adults. It is not intended for children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
